How a check works

Every file has a fingerprint, worked out from its exact bytes. Change one byte and the fingerprint changes.

Serey keeps every file on S5, its own storage. Posts saved with Forever are also kept on Arweave, a public network with no delete.

Your browser downloads each copy and works out its fingerprint again. Same fingerprint, same file. A file from your device is checked the same way, and is never uploaded.
